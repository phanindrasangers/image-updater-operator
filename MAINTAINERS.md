## Becoming a maintainer

The project is looking for additional maintainers. To be considered, sustained contribution to the codebase, reviews, issue triage, or documentation is expected. Open an issue or reach out to an existing maintainer to discuss.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Triage issues and review pull requests in a timely manner.
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Participate in release decisions and roadmap planning.
- Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) when handling vulnerability reports.
